- Headline (en): Tree plantation programme held at Jagran Institute of Management and Mass Communication (JIMMC)

## Summary (en)
The Kanpur Income Tax and GST Bar Association, with the Forest Department, held a tree
plantation programme at JIMMC, Kanpur, to promote environmental conservation and awareness.
