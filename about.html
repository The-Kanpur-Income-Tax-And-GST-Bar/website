About us

The registered association of Income Tax and GST practitioners in Kanpur.

The Kanpur Income Tax and GST Bar Association (Regd.) gives advocates and tax consultants who practise Income Tax and GST law in Kanpur a common platform.

The association holds knowledge-sharing programmes for members and passes important notices on to them. Every event, notice and photograph is published on this website.

What we do

Webinars and seminars

Discussions with expert speakers on new developments in Income Tax and GST, such as the decisions of the GST Council meetings.

Community programmes

Programmes such as plantation drives, in which members take part together.
